## What this is

A notebook kept across cities. I don't quit and travel — I keep and live well. Same work, different places. This site tries to make that visible.

## Two frames

Location-independent life comes in two frames. The loud one you've seen — quit your job, sell a course, chase sunsets. And a quieter one, harder to find written down. This is the quieter one. Middle budget. Real work. A small apartment. Morning walks.

## The triangle — eat, stay, work

Wherever I am, my day reads through three corners.

- **Eat** — what and where and with whom.
- **Stay** — the room I sleep in and how comfortable it is.
- **Work** — what I'm building and how I do it.

Three corners hold up a triangle. Same shape, different cities. Fukuoka, for example:

## The fourth dimension — routine

The triangle is the "what". The fourth dimension is the "how repeated".

Walking the same street to the same sandwich shop every morning. Watching a sidewalk get rebuilt over a week — first the digging, then the frame, then the pour, then the polish. This is routine. Not comfort. Attention.

Attention is what stops a foreign city from feeling foreign.

## What this is not

Not a guide. Not a course. Not "how to quit your job in 90 days". No sponsored posts, no affiliate links, no gated content.

## What this is

A working notebook, published as a book. Chapters land as they get written. Read straight through, or jump around — the frame holds either way.

The first city is Japan. Others come as I move.

— Oğulcan
